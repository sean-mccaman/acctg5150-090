SUMMIT GEAR CO.
Accounting Policy Memo AP-07: Allowance for Doubtful Accounts
Effective: December 1, 2024 (restates the practice in place since the ERP go-live, January 1, 2024)
Owner: Controller

Policy
1. The allowance for doubtful accounts is 2 percent of gross trade receivables at each
   quarter-end. The rate reflects the write-off history of the predecessor system.
2. A receivable is written off at the first month-end on which it is 180 or more days
   past due. The remaining balance is charged to the allowance. A later receipt on a
   written-off invoice is credited back to the allowance; there were none in the
   periods this ledger covers.
3. Each quarter-end, bad debt expense (the provision) is recorded for the amount that
   brings the allowance to 2 percent of gross receivables after that quarter's
   write-offs.
4. The Controller reviews the rate annually.
